# Attack round

1. Attacker spends 1 AP to roll for an attack. Rolled 4, 6, 2, 4 -> 3 successes
2. Defender spends 1 AP to roll to parry. Rolled 3, 5, 2 -> 1 success
3. The Attacker rolls a 1D20 to determine which body part he hits. Attacker rolls a 4 -> Left leg.
4. 1 success goes through and that means the attack was successful and the attacker rolls their weapon damage 1d6 -> 4
5. Since the defender has armor of 1 on their left leg only 3 damage goes through.

If the final success count was 2 the attacker would be able to choose one of the specials (bleed, choose a target location etc..). Attackers are granted a special every 2 successes. Meaning they can do multiple specials in one attacks if the target success number goes thrue.
That also goes in the opposite direction when the defender successfully paries and attack and rolls more than 2 successes over the attacker then they can use one of the defensive specials (disarm opponent, prepare counter, etc..).
