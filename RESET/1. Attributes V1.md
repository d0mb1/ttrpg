### Strength
Increases carrying capacity and the ability to use physical based weapons.

### Dexterity
Increases speed and the ability to use dexterity based weapons.

### Constitution
Increases max health and ovelall toughness. 

### Intelligence
Increases the ability to memorize spells and the ability to use intelligence based magic.

### Wisdom
Increases speed and the ability to use wisdom based magic.

### Charisma
Increases luck and the ability to deceive, convince, intimidate etc. people.
