- Might (strength) (physical strength)
- Agility (Dexterity) (physical agility)
- Intellect (Inteligence) (mental strength)
- Wits (Wisdom / Charisma) (mental agility)

Attributes tie in to skills giving them re-rolls based on the Attribute level.
- Might -> Athletics, Intimidation, Crafting, etc..
- Agility -> Stealth, Thievery, Acrobatics, etc..
- Intellect -> Persuasion, Concentration, History, etc..
- Wits -> Perception, Survival, Medicine, etc..

| Attribute LVL | 1 - 2 | 3 - 4 | 5 - 6 | 7 - 8 |
| ------------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Luck points   | 1     | 2     | 3     | 4     |

Level of an attribute also limits the max level of a skill.

| Attribute LVL   | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   | 6   | 7   | 8   |
| --------------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max skill level | 2   | 4   | 6   | 8   | 10  | 12  | 14  | 16  |
