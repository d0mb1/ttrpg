### Flanking

While flanking the the enemy the attacker gains +1 success to their rolls. In order to flank the enemy there has to be one hero standing behind and one standing on front of the enemy.

### Up against the wall

If a character has a wall behind him and someone is attacking him the attacker has +1 to their success. Essentially the same as flanking but the wall acts as a second player.

### Full and half cover

Full cover gives the ranged attacker -2 successes to their attack roll. For example standing behind a corner of a building. While behind a half cover ranged attacker has -1 success to their attack roll. For example hiding behind a box.
