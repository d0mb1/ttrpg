Accidental Injury: The defender deflects or twists an opponent’s attack in such a way that the attacker fumbles, injuring themself. The attacker must roll damage against himself in a random Hit Location using the weapon used to strike. If unarmed, the attacker tears or breaks something internal, the damage roll ignoring any armour.

Arise: Allows the defender to use a momentary opening to roll back up to their feet.

Bash: The attacker pushes the defender 1 square back for each success going through. If the defender hits an obstacle they have to roll a Athletics or Acrobatics check equal or higher to the attackers success to not fall prone for one turn. If the defender hits another combatant they also have to roll that check.

Bleed: If the attacker deals damage to the defender at the start of Grip: If the attacker has at least one hand free they can grip the defender and prevent them from disengaging. The defender then can try to break free by rolling against the attackers strength.

Circumvent Parry: Attacker can bypass a successful parry.

Impale: The attacker can roll twice for their damage and choose the better roll. If the weapon deals damage the attacker has the option of leaving the weapon in the wound or yanking it free on their next turn. While the defender is impaled he has -2 success to all rolls. When the attacker wants to yank the weapon out of the defender, they have to use an action. The defender may resist which which would lead to a strength check for both. If successful the attacker deals half of his weapons damage ignoring armor.

Marksman: The attacker can move the targeted area by one step the an adjacent area.

Maximise damage: The attacker automatically uses the maximum value on one of his damage dice as the damage delt.

Over-penetration: If the attacker deals damage to the defender the projectile pierces through the defender and hit an additional target behind him dealing half damage.

Rapid reload: The attacker is able to instantly reload his weapon without expending AP.

Scar foe: The combatant inflicts his opponent with a scar that will be the talk of all for the rest of his or her life.

Select target: The defender may deflect the attackers blow in a way so that it hits a different target adjacent the the attacker. The target is taken by surprise and is unable to react.

Slip free: On a Critical the defender can automatically escape being Entangled, Gripped, or Pinned.

Stun location: The attacker can use a bludgeoning weapon to temporarily stun the body part struck. If the blow overcomes Armour Points and injures the target, the defender must make an Opposed Roll of Endurance vs. the original attack roll. If the defender Fails, then the Hit Location is Incapacitated for a number of Turns equal to the damage inflicted. A blow to the torso causes the defender to stagger winded, only able to defend. A head shot renders the foe briefly insensible and unable to act.

Sunder: The attacker is able to damage the defenders armor. The defender armor now acts as HP that can be lowered or completely destroyed by the attacker.

Trip opponent: The character attempts to overbalance or throw his opponent to the ground. The opponent must make an Opposed Roll of his dexterity against the character’s original roll. If the target Fails, they fall prone.
