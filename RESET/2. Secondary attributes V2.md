
### Max health

Health is derived from constitution. There isn't a singular health pool but several - one for each body part: head, torso, 2x arm, gut and 2x leg. Health pool does not end at 0 rather it goes to the negative number of max health. After passing 0 in to the negatives the player will receive a penalty depending on which body part went under 0.

Health calculation: 10 + 2 * Might
The total HP pool will be destributes to all of the body parts.

- Head -> total HP / 5
- Arms -> total HP / 5
- Chest -> total HP / 3
- Abdomen -> total HP / 4
- Legs -> total HP / 4

That would mean that at level 1 of might the total HP pool is 12 which would translate to:

- Head -> 2 HP
- Arms (each) -> 2 HP
- Chest -> 4 HP
- Abdomen -> 3 HP
- Legs (each) -> 3 HP

Another alternative would be having just a static max health for all humans the same. And it wouldn't change with levels.

### Damage bonus

Depending on the weapon used and the level of Attribute the character might get a damage bonus to their weapon damage roll.

| LVL | 1 - 2 | 3 - 4 | 5 - 6 | 7 - 8 |
| --- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| DMG | 0     | +1    | +2    | +3    |

### Movement speed

Move distance per turn. Base is 5 squares per turn plus a bonus based on Agility. When wearing armor speed gets reduced.

| LVL   | 1 - 2 | 3 - 4 | 5 - 6 | 7 - 8 |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Speed | 0     | +1    | +2    | +3    |

### Initiative bonus

To base initiative rolls at the start of combat (d20) the player will add a modifier based on Agility level + Wits level

### Action points

Base action points start at 2 AP. AP can be used for attacks, movement and reactions during combat. To get more AP the players will have to upgrade their Agility and/or Wits.

- base AP + 1 -> player needs 4+ in Agility OR Wits
- base AP + 2 -> player need 6+ in Agility AND Wits

### Carry capacity

Determines how many kilograms the character is able to carry on them. The calculation: 5 + Might * 2

- Minor Encumbrance (> Carry capacity) - reduced speed, -1 stamina
- Major Encumbrance (> Carry capacity * 1,5) - halved, -2 stamina

Nobody ever cares about carry capacity so not sure if this is relevant.

### Energy

Energy determines how much energy the hero has available. When depleting all energy the hero falls unconscious. Base energy is 5 and scales with Might and Intellect. Every energy lost results in a d6 penalty die. Heroes loose energy while fighting in heavy armor or casting difficult spells.

| (Might + Intellect) / 2 | 1 - 2 | 3 - 4 | 5 - 6 | 7 - 8 |
| ----------------------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Energy bonus            | +0    | +1    | +2    | +3    |
