### Luck

Luck is equal to the players charisma. Players can spend luck to reroll any one roll. Luck is restored to it's max after a good night sleep.

### Carrying capacity

Carrying capacity is equal to the players strength times 10. The resulting number is the number of kilograms the player can hold.

### Speed

Speed in equal to the sum of dexterity and wisdom devided by 2. Speed then determines the number of AP (action points) that players can use during combat and the initiative at the start of combat.

| Speed | 1-2 | 3-5 | 6-8 | 9-11 | 12-13 |
| ----- | --- | --- | --- | ---- | ----- |
| AP    | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5    | 6     |

### Max health

Health is derived from constitution. There isn't a singular health pool but several - one for each body part: head, torso, arms, gut and legs. Health pool does not end at 0 rather it goes to the negative number of max health. After passing 0 in to the negatives the player will receive a penalty depending on which body part went under 0.

| Constitution Level | Head | Chest | Arms | Gut | Legs |
| ------------------ | ---- | ----- | ---- | --- | ---- |
| Base Health        | 5    | 10    | 7    | 5   | 10   |
| Scaling Factor     | 1    | 2     | 1.5  | 1   | 2    |
| 1                  | 6    | 12    | 8    | 6   | 12   |
| 13                 | 18   | 36    | 26   | 18  | 36   |
