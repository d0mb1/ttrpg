Unlike in DnD skills aren't derived from attributes. Skills still use attributes for adding dice together but the players choose which attribute they use for their skill roll as long as it makes sense. Example: Intimidation is purely a charisma skill in DnD but here players can say they have high strength so they will flex their muscles to intimidate and use their strength attribute. The possibility of skills is endless.
