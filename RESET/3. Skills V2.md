****Skills have to be specified

There are also social skills: Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation
These skills can be used with any of the Attributes as long as it makes sense.

Successes can be interpretted in different ways. One of which is just counting how many successes the player rolled and based on the difficulty of the given task determine the number of successes needed to pass the check. The other way and more often used is a fixed threshold of successes needed to be successful in the given task.
