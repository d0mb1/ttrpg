Every piece of equipment has an Encumbrance stat attached to it. This stat determines how much weight will the heroes be carrying around while in combat. The players will add up all the Encumbrance on their character sheet. Every third combat round the players will have to roll an endurance check to determine is they loose energy. For each energy lost the players will have to roll penalty dice (d6) for each energy lost. The target number of successes needed is determined by the amount of Encumbrance.

| Encumbrance added up | 0-9 | 10-19 | 20-29 | 30-39 | 40-49 | 50-59 |
| -------------------- | --- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Successes needed     | 0   | 1     | 2     | 3     | 4     | 5     |
