### Success

Whenever the player rolls some combination of dice they look at how many successes they have rolled. The more successes the better the roll.

After rolling the player counts how many dice have the value of 4 or more. They count the number of dice with that value and that will be the number of successes rolled.

Whenever the player rolls 8 or more on a die that roll will count as 2 successes.

Example: player rolls 3, 5, 6, 2, 9 -> that will add up to 4 successes.
