### Initiative

Initiative determines the order in which players and monsters take their turns. At the start of every combat all players and monsters roll 1D20 and add their speed to the roll to determine their initiative. 

### Round

Combat round is a complete cycle in combat during which all players and monsters take their turns in order of initiative, using their available Action Points (AP) to perform actions such as moving, attacking, or using abilities. After reaching the bottom of the initiative order, the round returns to the top, allowing participants with remaining AP to continue taking actions. This process repeats until no one has any AP left. Only then is all AP replenished for the next round.
