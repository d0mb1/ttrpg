# Actions

Players can preform actions during their turn.

### Attacking

When players want to preform an attack action they have to spend 1 AP. After spending 1 AP the player rolls dice depending on the level of the relevant the attribute depending on the weapon. The players can also add their skill dice if they are skilled in that particular weapon.

Based on the number of successes rolled the player rolls that many damage dice depending on the weapon.

Attacking multiple times with one weapon during a single turn will result in a -1 success penalty for each consecutive roll.

### Moving

Players can spend 1 AP to move 5 meters / squares + movement from their speed in their turn. Each consecutive movement action in a single turn will result in a 1 meter penalty.

### Pushing

Player can spend 1 AP to push their opponent. Both the player and the enemy roll their strength dice. If the player rolls more successes they'll push the enemy that many meters / squares depending on how many more successes they rolled.

### Blocking

When holding a weapon or a shield the player can choose a location which to passively protect. If the attacker hits the protected area the attack has -1 success when defender is blocking with a weapon. Shields can protect multiple places at ones and have their ows stats for negating successes. While blocking the player cannot use the weapon or the shield to parry or to attack. 

# Reactions

Players can preform reaction during other players or monsters turns under the specific reaction conditions.

### Parrying

If a player is being attacked by a physical weapon (including ranged weapons) they can spend 1 AP to parry the attackers attack by rolling an opposed roll with their weapon skill dice subctracting the rolled successes.
