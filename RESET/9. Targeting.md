One possibility of targeting specific body parts is when rolling for an attack. After rolling the player can choose any of the numbers rolled to determine which bodypart they'll be hitting.

V1

| Bodypart      | Head  | Torso | Gut   | Arms  | Legs |
| ------------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ---- |
| Number rolled | 1, 12 | 5 - 8 | 2, 11 | 3, 10 | 4, 9 |
V2

| Bodypart      | Head | Torso    | Gut | Arms | Legs |
| ------------- | ---- | -------- | --- | ---- | ---- |
| Number rolled | 8    | the rest | 7   | 5    | 6    |

The other possibility would be to create a system where players choose which bodypart they'll be hitting beforehand and depending on which bodypart it is they'll get a success penalty.

| Bodypart        | Head | Torso | Gut | Arms | Legs |
| --------------- | ---- | ----- | --- | ---- | ---- |
| Success penalty | -3   | 0     | -2  | -1   | -1   |
